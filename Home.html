Who We Are

Two guys who
actually show
up for you.

TNC Builders is Rob Tienken and Logan Cunningham — a tight-knit two-man team who believe that building something great starts with listening. From the first conversation to the final walkthrough, you'll find us responsive, transparent, and genuinely invested in getting it right.

Whether it's a quick repair or the deck you've been dreaming about for years, Rob and Logan bring the same care to every job. We flag ways to save money, return unused materials, and keep you in the loop throughout — because a project that's stress-free for you is a project we're proud of.

How We Work

The TNC standard

01
Your Budget Is Sacred
Unused materials get returned and refunded — no exceptions. We'd rather you come in under budget and send your neighbors our way than quietly pad a bill.
02
You'll Never Have to Chase Us
We stay proactively in touch and respond fast — because wondering where your project stands is the last thing you should be dealing with.
03
Every Job Gets Our Best
A small repair gets the same attention as a full custom build. We don't have a B-team — just two guys who take pride in the work, every time.
04
We Build Where We Live
Rob is a new dad. We're both neighbors who live in the communities we work in. Job-site safety isn't a policy — it's personal.
05
Your Idea Stays Yours
We'll always look for smarter ways to build — but never at the cost of what makes your vision yours. Design first. Cost engineering second.
06
We Care How It Turns Out
This isn't just a job. We care about the finished product the way you do — because your home deserves people who lose sleep over the details.
